# Neon Neko Café

A cyberpunk cat girl café idle clicker. Pet Kiko, hire a neon crew of cat girls, and build a café empire across six districts.

**Play it: https://kiwigeek.github.io/neon-neko-cafe/**

| Edition | Where it runs |
|---|---|
| HTML5 | In your browser, straight away |
| MS-DOS | In your browser through DOSBox-X (WebAssembly), with a choice of VGA, EGA, CGA, Hercules or Tandy graphics and Sound Blaster, AdLib, Gravis Ultrasound, Disney, PC speaker, Tandy, MT-32 or General MIDI sound. Also downloadable for a real 386+ PC or DOSBox. |
| Windows | Download: one EXE for Windows 95 through 11, no installer, no DirectX |

## Downloads

- [NeonNekoCafe-Windows.zip](downloads/NeonNekoCafe-Windows.zip) — Windows 95 to 11
- [NeonNekoCafe-DOS.zip](downloads/NeonNekoCafe-DOS.zip) — MS-DOS on a real PC (386 or better)
- [NeonNekoCafe-DOSBox.zip](downloads/NeonNekoCafe-DOSBox.zip) — MS-DOS with ready-made DOSBox configs
